One-time health data backfill

This reads the 90-day export at _health-backfill-temp.json (in this same repo) and sends it to /api/health-intake on this site — the normal sync endpoint, just a one-off manual trigger instead of the usual automated phone sync.

Click once. Safe to click more than once if something goes wrong — ingestion just merges data in, it won't duplicate or wipe anything.
